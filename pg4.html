What would a Cookie-less world look like?

Restrict, Restrict, Restrict (Care Ethics Lens)

  • Restricting cookies would be a nice middle ground between the current lawless landscape of the US and the EU.
  • Obviously, part of the drawback is that it actually needs to be decided where to draw the line
  • Assuming exclusively user function was not affected, companies would not be allowed to buy and sell data through the use of internet cookies
  • Users are safe from predatory cookies-
  • Developers can still make usable websites, but may not be able to make as much advertising money through them
  • No more targeted ads
