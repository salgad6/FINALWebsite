Are they Dangerous?

What Kind of Data?

  • Cookies can store all kinds of data on users, and then is usually combined with other data to create a profile on a user to tailor their experience. (Think forms, queries, emails, and webpage keywords)
  • Internet Cookies have a lot of concerns with data privacy. Innately, because how the potentially sensitive data that is stored, it is a question of Convenience vs Privacy in this case

Would you still use the internet the same way if you knew that any data that you could be tracked through it, not only online, but in real life too?

How is the Data used?

  • Targeted Advertising

    • Utilizing data about webpages that the user visits, advertisers will change their ads based on what think they you are more likely to buy or click on

    This has more than 1 side to it, targeted advertising isn’t inherently bad. It genuinely can be useful to find users the results that they actually want faster, but when it’s used in a predatory way, it preys on the user's fears and lack of understanding. (Think, targeted gambling ads. More likely to be served to someone with a gambling problem with little regard of ‘if they should’)

  • Risk Assessment/Background Checks

    • Survey data can sometimes be sold to background check websites and even appear in risk assessment like insurance

    As we discussed in class, background checks/risk assessment are often very biased. By including things like algorithms into these kinds of assessment, they are more likely to replicate human biases

Should you be allowed to be graded for your risk assessment based on your behavior on the internet?

Zombie Cookies, Flash, and Bad Practices

Zombie cookies are a type of (potentially malicious) cookie that is stored in multiple parts of your computer and cannot be removed by clearing the cache, unlike other cookies. These cookies will duplicate themselves if all copies are not removed.

Adobe Flash was a web player that let developers do things like animate, use vector graphics, and more. Its support from all major browsers was removed in 2021 because of its security risks. Cookies from Adobe Flash were not stored in the cache like most other cookies and did not have an expiration date by default.

Jet Blue Current lawsuit for Jet Blue for supposedly utilizing user cookie data for what is essentially surge pricing by an upwards of $230 USD. No further details on the lawsuit as of 5/13

Recent Malware Operation that stole over 90 billion cookies, some of which contained sensitive data (names, passwords, addresses, and more)
