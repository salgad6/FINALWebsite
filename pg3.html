A World Without Cookies?

To remove all Cookies would entirely remove the issue at hand. If companies cannot use your Cookie data legally, they will have to find a different way to gather user data for advertising. However, removing ALL Cookies would certainly break the functionality of a lot of websites and restrict what developers would be allowed to do with their websites Many websites also make money off of allowing ads to be played on their website (which may come with Cookies) Website Functionality Breaks Devs need to find a new way to make websites work with less security risks - A lot of websites no longer work, but eventually all of the pesky risks with browsing will disappear with it +/- Because the population of people that are “users” is significantly larger than the amount of companies or devs, I think that it is reasonable that ideals that align with the population of Users should be considered above profits for both developers and data brokers No more cookies or online tracking for buying/selling data -
