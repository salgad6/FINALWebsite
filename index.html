Hello, and Welcome to an Introduction to Cookies!

Internet Cookies are small bits of information stored on a user’s computer during and/or in between sessions, generally for the purpose of website functionality and advertising. Cookies have been discussed as a potential security risk with the data that they store being potentially sensitive and able to be used against users, creating several ethical issues surrounding internet privacy and user rights.
