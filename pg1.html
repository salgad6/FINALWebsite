What's a "Cookie" anyways?

Timeline of the history of internet cookies

  • Created by Lou Montulli in 1994, created to store small data on the user computer. It was originally intended for things like persistent logins.

Who is involved in the movement of data via cookies?

  • Users: Provide the data to use, most likely to be unaware and exploited, largest group
  • Buying/Selling Cookie Data: Collect, buy, sell, share user data gathered through cookies. Under some legal constraints (ie. Currently has law constraining them) that heavily favor the collectors and buyers
  • Developer Uses of Cookies: Utilize cookies for website functionality

So, What IS a Cookie?

Cookies are little bits of data stored on a user’s browser cache when they visit websites. Because the internet is international, the laws on their legality and what you are allowed to do with them varies. In the US, you only have to notify users that cookies are being enabled on a website. The EU requires you to manually opt in to cookie use. Data may be stored upon certain actions. Things like: adding items to a shopping cart, logging in, and navigating a website are common ways that cookies may be added.

(Some) Cookie Types, these types have overlap and this list is not exhaustive:

  • Session Cookies

    Cookies that go away after you close the page/browser.

  • Tracker

    This type of cookie usually is used for advertisement and follows you through as you browse the internet

  • Necessary & Functional

    Both of these types of cookies are used to ensure that the website functions as intended. This may also include functions like performance tracking
