Removing Cookies

Say goodbye to your friend :((

So. You might be wondering... "How can I protect myself?" Luckily, for the majority of cookies, you can remove them just by clearing your cache.

Just a warning, this will sign you out of everything, clear your shopping carts, etc. It can be helpful to look at a fresh browser without any tracking cookies (Thanks, Google.)

If you're on google, you should see two circles and bars near the URL of this webpage... Go to Cookies and Site Data

And now you can clear the cookies on just this page!
